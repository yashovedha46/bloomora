## COMPANY PROFILE

Company Name: 🌿 Bloomora 🪴<br>

Tagline: “Turning Dreams into Green Spaces.”<br>

### About Us<br>

Bloomora 🌿 is a green décor and plant-solutions company that transforms ordinary spaces into beautiful, refreshing, and eco-friendly environments. We bring our customers’ ideas to life through creative plant arrangements and customized green décor.<br>

### Vision<br>

To create greener, healthier, and more beautiful spaces by bringing people closer to nature through creative and eco-friendly plant solutions.<br>



### Mission<br>

To turn every space into a place where nature, creativity, and people can grow together.<br>



### Benefits & Application Areas 🪴<br>

1. 🏨 Hotels – Decorate reception areas, rooms, balconies, and common spaces with suitable plants.<br>


2. 🍽️ Restaurants & Cafés – Add small plants to dining tables, counters, entrances, and seating areas to create a fresh atmosphere.<br>


3. 🏫 Schools – Bring greenery into classrooms, corridors, libraries, and outdoor areas to create a pleasant learning environment.<br>


4. 🎓 Colleges – Decorate departments, classrooms, canteens, corridors, and student common areas with creative plant arrangements.<br>


5. 🏢 Offices – Add plants to reception desks, workspaces, meeting rooms, and waiting areas to create a refreshing atmosphere.<br>


6. 🎉 Event Venues & Parks – Use customized plant arrangements for weddings, celebrations, exhibitions, and other events.<br>


7. 🏠 Homes & Apartments – Create small green corners for balconies, living rooms, study areas, and entrances.<br>


8. 🏬 Shops & Commercial Spaces – Use plants to make entrances, display areas, and customer spaces more attractive.<br>


9. 🏘️ Residential Communities – Add greenery to common areas, entrances, walkways, and community spaces.<br>


10. 🌿 Special Occasions – Provide customized plant arrangements and plant-based gifts for birthdays, weddings, college functions, corporate events, and celebrations.<br>
